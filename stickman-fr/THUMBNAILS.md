## MINIATURE 1 — LES DEUX CHÈQUES

**Idée (FR) :** Léo brandit un chèque doré gigantesque pendant que le créateur au million s'effondre avec un chèque minuscule. Le paradoxe de l'accroche, en une image.

**Prompt :**

A maximum impact cinematic cartoon stick figure thumbnail built around one extreme contrast. COMPOSITION FROM LEFT TO RIGHT AND TOP TO BOTTOM: the left two thirds of the frame are owned by the main character Leo, standing in a wide triumphant power stance with feet planted far apart, body turned three quarters toward the viewer, both arms raised high above his head gripping an enormous glowing golden cheque that runs diagonally from his hands up into the top right area of the frame. Leo occupies roughly half the total frame height and his large round pure white head sits in the upper left third, the single most readable element in the image. His face is pushed to maximum expression: thick black eyebrows shot up to the very top of his head, eyes stretched wide with visible irises, mouth open enormously wide with teeth and tongue clearly visible in explosive disbelief. His costume is his locked look pushed sharp for the thumbnail: a heather grey hooded sweatshirt with white drawstrings worn open over a crisp bright white shirt, dark blue jeans rolled at the ankles, bright red sneakers with white soles, and electric blue over-ear headphones around his neck catching a hot specular highlight. In the lower right third, much smaller and lower in the frame, a second stick figure creator in an olive green bomber jacket and black trousers has collapsed to his knees, entire body slumped, head dropped forward, shoulders caving inward, arms hanging limp, holding a tiny dull grey cheque the size of a ticket in one loose hand, eyebrows curved into a deep sad arc, mouth pulled down and trembling. BACKGROUND ENVIRONMENT: a dramatic darkened studio interior pushed far harder than the video style, deep navy and near black walls with a violent burst of golden light exploding outward from directly behind Leo's raised cheque, radiating light rays streaking to all four corners, thick atmospheric haze catching every ray, a scatter of gold coins frozen mid air tumbling through the light, and cold blue rim light isolating the collapsed figure on the right so the two halves of the image read as gold versus grey. The floor is a glossy dark surface that mirrors the golden explosion in long vertical streaks. TEXT ELEMENTS: exactly two. First, in the upper centre right against the dark navy area, the words UN MILLION DE VUES in bold clean heavy sans serif capitals, pure white with a very thick black outline, set at a slight downward angle, deliberately separated from Leo's body. Second, in the lower left corner over the dark floor area, the words LE PLUS GROS CHÈQUE in bold clean heavy sans serif capitals, pure white with a very thick black outline, with the word CHÈQUE rendered noticeably larger in a rich gradient gold running from bright yellow at the top to deep orange at the bottom, so it reads as the single most important word in the image. ACCENT ELEMENT: one large high contrast bold arrow in saturated gradient red, thick and slightly curved, starting near the words UN MILLION DE VUES in the upper right and sweeping down and left to point directly at the tiny grey cheque held by the collapsed figure, creating an instant visual joke that the eye follows in under a second. READABILITY: everything is built to read at 200 pixels wide, no small details anywhere, Leo's raised arms and giant cheque form a clear silhouette, the two cheques sit at wildly different scales so the comparison is understood without reading a single word.

## MINIATURE 2 — LA PORTE VERROUILLÉE

**Idée (FR) :** Léo pousse de toutes ses forces contre une porte de fer géante à deux verrous, sous les torches, tandis que le compteur de vues explose de l'autre côté. La frustration du seuil de monétisation.

**Prompt :**

A maximum impact cinematic cartoon stick figure thumbnail built around one moment of total physical effort. COMPOSITION FROM LEFT TO RIGHT AND TOP TO BOTTOM: the main character Leo occupies the centre left of the frame at roughly half the total frame height, seen in a hard side on pushing stance, one shoulder driven into a colossal iron door on the right, both legs braced at a steep diagonal with his red sneakers skidding backward and kicking up dust clouds, torso rotated fully into the effort, both arms locked straight against the metal. His large round pure white head is turned toward the viewer in the upper left third, pushed to maximum expression: thick black eyebrows slammed down hard into a furious angle, eyes squeezed into determined narrow shapes with visible irises, mouth stretched wide open with teeth and tongue clearly visible in a straining shout. His costume is his locked look pushed sharp: a heather grey hooded sweatshirt with white drawstrings worn over a crisp white shirt, sleeves shoved up, dark blue jeans rolled at the ankles, bright red sneakers with white soles glowing against the dust, and electric blue over-ear headphones around his neck catching a hot orange highlight from the torches. BACKGROUND ENVIRONMENT: the right half of the frame is filled by an enormous riveted iron door set into a towering stone wall, pushed far darker and more saturated than the video style, with two massive engraved padlocks hanging at different heights catching hard orange firelight. Two flaming torches flank the door and throw violent flickering orange light and long hard shadows across every rivet, embers and sparks streaming upward through thick smoke haze. Above the wall the sky is a deep churning storm violet with a single crack of white lightning. Behind the top edge of the door a colossal cold white view counter glows through the seams, its light leaking around the door frame in hard blue blades that contrast against the orange firelight, showing that enormous numbers are happening on the unreachable side. TEXT ELEMENTS: exactly two. First, across the upper left area against the dark stormy sky, the words PAS POUR VOS VUES in bold clean heavy sans serif capitals, pure white with a very thick black outline, with the word VUES rendered noticeably larger in a rich gradient electric blue running from pale cyan at the top to deep blue at the bottom, matching the cold counter light and marking it as the key word. Second, in the lower right corner over the dark stone base of the wall, the words QUATRE MILLE HEURES in smaller bold clean sans serif capitals, pure white with a very thick black outline. Both text blocks are placed with strong empty separation from Leo's body so nothing collides. ACCENT ELEMENT: one large glowing high contrast circle in saturated gradient yellow drawn around the upper padlock, thick and slightly rough edged like a marker ring, with three short radiating impact strokes around it, pulling the eye instantly to the lock that is stopping everything. READABILITY: built to read at 200 pixels wide, Leo's pushing silhouette against the flat mass of the door is unmistakable, the dust clouds and skid marks communicate maximum effort with no small details required, and the orange versus blue split makes the two halves separate instantly.

## MINIATURE 3 — LE SOL ET LE SOMMET

**Idée (FR) :** Léo se tient sur un sol de pièces d'or, minuscule face à une montagne d'or qui monte hors du cadre. La publicité n'est que le plancher des revenus.

**Prompt :**

A maximum impact cinematic cartoon stick figure thumbnail built around one extreme scale contrast. COMPOSITION FROM LEFT TO RIGHT AND TOP TO BOTTOM: the main character Leo stands in the left third of the frame on a solid floor made entirely of packed gold coins, shot from a low dramatic angle so he rises large against the sky and occupies roughly half the total frame height. His body is in a wide open power stance, feet planted apart on the coins, one arm thrown straight up pointing at the sky, the other arm swept out wide, chest pushed forward, head tipped back and turned toward the viewer. His large round pure white head sits in the upper left third and is pushed to maximum expression: thick black eyebrows shot up high, eyes stretched enormously wide with visible irises, mouth open huge with teeth and tongue clearly visible in stunned realisation. His costume is his locked look pushed sharp for the thumbnail: a heather grey hooded sweatshirt with white drawstrings worn open over a crisp bright white shirt, dark blue jeans rolled at the ankles, bright red sneakers with white soles planted on the gold, and electric blue over-ear headphones around his neck catching a hot golden highlight. Behind it a sky of saturated orange, deep magenta, and violet with heavy layered clouds and long light rays breaking through, several loose coins tumbling through the air catching the light, and a low warm haze rolling across the coin floor at Leo's feet. The coin floor stretches from the bottom left corner to the base of the mountain, its texture bold and clearly readable, reflecting warm light in broad streaks.
